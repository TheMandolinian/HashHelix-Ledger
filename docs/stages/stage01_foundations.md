# Stage 1 — Foundations of the HashHelix Engine

Stage 1 establishes the core philosophy and computational primitives of the
HashHelix deterministic engine:

• Time is mathematical, not metadata.
• The engine must evolve through pure computation only.
• All future stages must obey deterministic sequencing.
• No consensus mechanisms, randomness, or probabilistic ordering.
• No transaction pools, validators, or forks.

Stage 1 formally defines the commitment to:
- reproducibility,
- engine purity,
- mathematical truth as the foundation of sequence evolution.
